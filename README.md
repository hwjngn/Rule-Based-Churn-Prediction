# Rule-Based Customer Retention Engine using SQL & PowerBI.

## Executive Summary
To address a 20.4% customer churn rate in the retail banking sector, I developed a proactive Customer Retention Engine using SQL and Power BI that shifted the organization from reactive reporting to forward-looking, risk-based intervention. The solution transforms raw customer behavior into a transparent, weighted risk scorecard that surfaces key attrition drivers such as product concentration, inactivity among high-balance users, and demographic risk patterns. Customers are then segmented into clear, actionable risk tiers to support targeted retention decisions. As a result, the approach:

1. Replaced inefficient, random targeting with a prioritized, data-driven Hit List.
2. Achieved a 69% precision rate in the Critical segment, representing a 3.4x lift over the baseline.
3. Enabled the bank to focus limited retention resources on customers most likely to churn, helping protect revenue and improve customer lifetime value.
