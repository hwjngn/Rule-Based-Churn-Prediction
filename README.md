# Rule-Based Customer Retention Engine using SQL & PowerBI.
A rule-based risk management tool designed to combat customer attrition. By transforming raw banking data into a weighted scorecard, this project identifies a 'Critical' segment with a 69% churn rate—a 3.4x improvement over the 20% baseline. It provides stakeholders with an actionable 'Hit List' for proactive retention.

## Executive Summary
To address a 20.4% customer churn rate in retail banking, I built a proactive Customer Retention Engine using SQL and Power BI that shifted the organization from reactive reporting to predictive intervention. The solution converts raw customer behavior into a transparent, weighted risk scorecard that highlights key attrition drivers and segments customers into clear risk tiers.

By replacing broad, random targeting with a data-driven Hit List, the model achieved a 69% precision rate in the Critical segment, representing a 3.4x lift over the baseline. This enabled the bank to focus limited retention resources on customers most likely to churn, helping protect revenue and improve customer lifetime value.
